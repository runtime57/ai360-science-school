# Reading Papers

This section provides helpful guidance on reading papers and taking your first steps in research.

## Getting Started

The first step in any research project, once you have defined its general direction, is to review the literature and develop an intuition for the field—in this case, NLP.

Start with these two documents:

1. [Writing Scientific Papers and Reports](https://github.com/Blinorot/pytorch_project_template/blob/report/HowToWriteAPaper.pdf) by [Petr Grinberg](https://github.com/Blinorot)

   This document explains the purpose of each section of a paper, what to consider when planning your research and experiments, and where to find the information you need.

2. [Reading Scientific Papers](https://disk.yandex.ru/i/ipDmnu4_qJ0oqQ) by [Max Kaledin](https://github.com/XuMuK1)

   This document offers a plan for writing a short summary of each paper and explains which information is important and why. Although writing summaries can be time-consuming, it helps you understand which datasets to use, which baselines to choose, and so on.

## Exploring the Literature

The best way to start exploring the literature in a new domain is to read a few survey papers. They describe the main approaches and the most popular and relevant datasets. Surveys help you develop an intuitive understanding of the field and see how researchers arrived at the methods widely used today.

After that, explore a few papers that are particularly relevant to your task. ChatGPT or another LLM can help you choose them. As mentioned above, remember to write a short summary of each paper.
